Let's add the simplest possible device — a **GPIO switch** — and control it
from the dashboard. A GPIO switch drives one output pin: a relay board, a
MOSFET module, or just the board's LED for a first test.

<Steps>

1. Before wiring anything, open **Settings → Controller board** and pick your
   exact board/chip model (classic ESP32, S2, S3, C3, or C6, and the specific
   variant if it's listed). Pin availability — which GPIOs exist, which are
   ADC/strapping/input-only, which are reserved — differs per board, and every
   device form's pin picker uses this selection to show only pins your board
   actually has.

2. Open **Devices** in the portal and click the add-device button.

   ![Device type selection](../../../assets/screenshots/portal-device-create.png)

3. Pick **GPIO Switch** from the catalog.

4. Fill in the settings:

   ![GPIO switch settings](../../../assets/screenshots/device-gpio-switch.png)

   - **Name** — whatever you like, e.g. `Test LED`.
   - **GPIO pin** — the pin your load is wired to (GPIO 2 drives the onboard
     LED on many dev boards).
   - **Inverted** — check this if your relay board is active-low (very common).
   - **Startup state** and **Restore previous state** — what the output should
     do right after a reboot: a fixed state, or whatever it was before power
     was lost.
   - **Safe state** — the state the output falls back to if the device that
     controls it becomes unavailable.

5. Save. The device appears in the list, reaches **ready**, and shows a live
   on/off toggle — click it and your relay clicks.

6. Pin it to the dashboard: open **Panels**, add a switch widget for your new
   device, and place it where you want. From now on it is one tap from the
   landing page.

</Steps>

## Where to go from here

- Wire a **DS18B20 temperature probe**: create a
  [1-Wire bus](/gekko/reference/devices/onewire-bus/) on a free GPIO, scan it,
  and add the [probe](/gekko/reference/devices/ds18b20/) the scan found.
- Put the switch on a [schedule](/gekko/reference/devices/schedule/) with an
  [auto switch](/gekko/guides/schedules-and-automation/).
- Keep temperature stable with a
  [thermostat](/gekko/reference/devices/thermostat/) driving your switch.
- Show it all on an OLED with the
  [display designer](/gekko/guides/displays/).
