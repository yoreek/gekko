## What is a DS18B20?

The DS18B20 is *the* hobby temperature sensor: a digital probe, a few dollars
in a waterproof stainless sleeve, accurate to ±0.5 °C. Being digital matters —
the temperature is measured inside the sensor and travels as data, so cable
length and contact resistance don't skew the reading the way they do with
analog sensors. Each probe also carries a factory-burned unique address, which
is what lets **many probes share one wire**: tank water, sump, room air — all
on a single GPIO.

The wiring, the pull-up resistor, and how addressing works are covered on the
[1-Wire bus page](/gekko/reference/devices/onewire-bus/) — read that first if
the bus is new to you.

## Setting it up: scan, don't type

1. Create a **1-Wire bus** device on the GPIO your probes are wired to.
2. Create a **DS18B20** device, select that bus as its dependency, and press
   **Scan dependency** — Gekko searches the bus and lists every probe it
   found. Pick your probe from the *scan candidate* list; its address fills in
   automatically.

![DS18B20 settings with the scan candidate picker](../../../../assets/screenshots/device-ds18b20.png)

A healthy DS18B20 scans with **family code 28**, a 16-hex-character address,
and no CRC flag.

:::tip[Which probe is which?]
With several probes on the bus, scan once, then warm one probe in your hand
and watch which reading moves — label the wire, repeat. Do it before zip-tying
anything.
:::

## Watching it: live values and charts

The sensor reports its temperature with a validity flag — a disconnected or
CRC-failing probe shows as *invalid*, never as a stale number. Click its tile
on the dashboard and you get the live value with a history chart:

![Water temperature dialog with live history chart](../../../../assets/screenshots/device-ds18b20-history.png)

The same reading feeds everything else in Gekko:

- a [thermostat](/gekko/reference/devices/thermostat/) controlling a heater or
  chiller;
- [display placeholders](/gekko/guides/displays/) like
  `{{dev.<id>.temperature | fixed:1}}` on an OLED;
- Home Assistant as a read-only `sensor` entity on
  [MQTT builds](/gekko/guides/mqtt-home-assistant/).

## Configuration

| Field | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `address` | — | The probe's ROM address — take it from the scan |
| `resolution` | `12` | 9–12 bits: 12 = 0.0625 °C steps but ~750 ms conversions; 9 = coarser and fast |
| `unit` | `celsius` | Display unit |
| `pollMs` | `5000` | How often to read the probe |
| `reportDeltaCelsius` | `0.01` | Minimum change before a new reading is pushed |
| `reportAlways` | off | Push every poll regardless of the delta |

Water temperature moves slowly — the default 5 s poll with a small report
delta keeps the WebSocket and history quiet without missing anything real.
